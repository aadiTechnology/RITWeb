School On Web...
www.RITeSchool.com is mainly developed to share the burden of School Administration, Teachers and to give comfort to Parents.
RITeSchool assists schools in following ways:
  • Automatic generation of -
        Muster Report, All Mark Sheets, Progress Reports, Exam Results, Annual Reports.
  • Time table creation along with a global view of school timetable. This assists schools to manage teacher and
        class allocations in an effective manner.
  • Message center helps schools improve communication with parents. E.g. it can send single message to the
        entire school and parents of kids studying in the school. All sent and received messages can be viewed in one
        place. The address book is easily available hence no need to type names of receivers.
  • SMS Center - For quick information circulation, school authority can send SMS to all teachers/parents.
  • Fee collection - Automatic calculation of late fees, printable version of receipts, acceptance of
        post-dated cheques and payment of fees against them.
  • Library Management to help the librarian to manage books.
  • Next academic year data generation - All the passed students will automatically be transferred to the next
        standard along with their data.
  • Image & Video gallery feature serves as a repository for the school’s favorite images and videos. School
        administration can control the content before publishing.
  • Notice board to send important messages to entire school to be immediately viewed on login. Helps to convey
        important message to individual parents as well.
  • Teacher allocation – Allocating teacher's classes/lectures/timetable to another teacher. Very tedious task
        made simple in fraction of a seconds.
  • Student division transfer – Student(s) can be easily transferred from one division to another along with
        his/her entire data (marks, attendance etc.)

    RITeSchool is for parents too…
  • Parents can see the daily Attendance Report instead of just the total present days.
  • Pending/Paid fees status can be tracked easily.
  • Exam marks are readily available once school publishes them.
  • Detailed Exam schedule is displayed test wise along with starting date - ending date for each exam to be
        held in the year.
  • The Message Center helps parents and teachers communicate better.
  • Annual Planner helps parents to know the activities that the school will conduct in the year.
  • Holidays declared by school are displayed on site for easy reference. No need to look into school diary
        each time.
  • Student/Parent Profile Management - Parents can update changes in their mobile phones / other contact
        details any time. No need to contact school for minor updates.
